# XP & Progression System Design

**Design Brief:** Based on the provided task specification (ID: e0f2c870, Project: df2f0007), this document outlines a coherent player loop, onboarding, progression logic, social motivation strategy, and verifiable acceptance criteria for an XP system that is cosmetic-only, transparent, and non-competitive.

---

## 1. Player Loop

| Phase | Action | XP Reward | Outcome |
|-------|--------|-----------|---------|
| **Onboarding** | Complete tutorial zone, interact with 3 objects, win 1 minigame | 50 + 75 + 100 = 225 XP | Unlock "Novice" title, basic cosmetic (hat) |
| **Core Loop** | Visit a new zone → interact with environment → play a minigame → exit | Zone: 50 XP<br>Interaction: 25 XP<br>Minigame win: 100 XP<br>Minigame participation: 50 XP | XP bar fills; level-up triggers when bar reaches threshold |
| **Reward Claim** | Level-up → claim cosmetic/title → outfit character | Variable (see Reward Design) | Visible change in avatar UI/profile |

*XP Bar Threshold:* Linear scale of **100 XP per level** (Level 1: 0–99, Level 2: 100–199, etc.). This ensures transparency—players always know exactly how much XP is needed for the next level.

---

## 2. Onboarding Flow

1. **First Launch** → Tutorial prompts appear, guiding player through zone visit, object interaction, and minigame.
2. **First 225 XP Earned** → Automatic level-up to **Level 2**, "Novice" title applied, "Rustic Cap" cosmetic unlocked and equipped.
3. **Profile Tutorial** → Player sees their XP counter, level number, and a "View Rewards" button. No hidden mechanics are introduced.

*Evidence:* The onboarding sequence directly maps to the "XP awarded for zone visits, interactions, and minigames" acceptance criterion.

---

## 3. Reward Design

### Cosmetic Items
- **Titles:** Displayed under player username (e.g., "Novice", "Explorer", "Champion"). No effect on gameplay stats.
- **Outfit Pieces:** Hats, capes, color palettes, emotes. Stored in a wardrobe UI; can be swapped at any time.
- **Badge Icons:** Small icons next to player name in social hubs.

### Robux Purchase Option
- **Available:** Small cosmetic bundles (e.g., "Starter Pack" – 1 title + 1 outfit piece) for Robux.
- **Not Available:** No purchase that grants XP, skips levels, or provides gameplay advantages.
- **Progression Integrity:** The free earnable path is identical to the Robux path. Purchased items are purely decorative and do not appear in progression logs or level-up rewards.

*Evidence:* Acceptance criterion "Robux purchase option exists but does not unlock exclusive progression" is satisfied by separating cosmetic sales from XP/progression mechanics.

---

## 4. Progression Transparency & Verification

**Player Profile Display:**
- Current XP / XP needed for next level (e.g., `245 / 100`)
- Current level number
- Reward history log (timestamped entries of earned titles/cosmetics)
- "How I earned my XP" expandable list showing each activity and XP amount

**XP Log Entries (Example):**
- `[11:20] Visited "Sunset Plaza"` → +50 XP
- `[11:23] Interacted with fountain` → +25 XP
- `[11:30] Won "Obby Challenge"` → +100 XP

**Leaderboard Design:**
- Based on **total playtime** and **creative contributions**, not level number or XP count.
- Levels/titles shown as engagement badges, not power indicators.

*Evidence:* Meets "Progression is transparent and verifiable" by providing open XP counters, logs, and no hidden RNG in core level advancement.

---

## 5. Acceptance Criteria Checklist

| # | Criterion | How It's Met |
|---|-----------|--------------|
| 1 | XP is awarded for zone visits, interactions, and minigames | Defined XP amounts per activity (50, 25, 100, 50) |
| 2 | Level-up triggers a visible change (e.g., new outfit, title) | Automatic title application + cosmetic unlock on level-up; displayed in avatar UI |
| 3 | Rewards are cosmetic only and do not provide gameplay advantages | All rewards are outfit/title/badge items; no stats, buffs, or power changes |
| 4 | Robux purchase option exists but does not unlock exclusive progression | Robux buys cosmetic bundles only; progression XP/level curve is identical for all players |
| 5 | Progression is transparent and verifiable | Player profile shows XP bar, needed amount, log of entries; no hidden mechanics |

---

## 6. Limitations & Design Notes

- This is a **design proposal** based on the brief and acceptance criteria. No actual code, assets, or service integration is claimed unless explicitly developed.
- XP amounts and level formula are intentionally simple (linear 100 XP/level) to ensure immediate transparency; alternative scaling (e.g., exponential) can be explored later but would require re-verifying criterion #5.
- "Meaningful rewards" are defined here as cosmetic items and titles that reflect player engagement without altering competitive balance.
- Robux integration assumes a pre-existing currency system; the design isolates its impact to avoid unintended gameplay effects.
- Social motivation relies on displayable cosmetics/titles and playtime-based leaderboards, avoiding pay-to-win perceptions.
